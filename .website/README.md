WORD5
===

**Five word games every day, five letters max per word !**

Hi, this is a bonus entry!

It's a compression challenge and an homage to NYT's daily word games.

The main goal here was to fit a huge dictionary in less than 13kb:

The game contains all 14,915 English words between 2 and 5 letters long, initially weighing 83kb, and carefully crafted to fit into 11,097 bytes !

The remaining bytes were used to implement 5 daily word games and compute their solutions:
- **Wordle** (type 5-letter words, orange shows misplaced letters, green shows good letters)
- **Word mix** (find all the words that can be written with 5 different letters)
- **Boggle** (find words made of 3 to 5 adjacent letters on a 4x4 grid)
- **Super word** (find all words containing a 3-letter string)
- **Maxi word** (find the longest words in the lines and columns of a 5x5 grid)

All these games let you play with words up to 5 letters long which are present in the dictionary.
Your scores can be shared online, and you can come back every day for new challenges !
Oh, and it's mobile-friendly !
